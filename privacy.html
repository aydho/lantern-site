Lantern Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Lantern is an inspector for Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. It runs entirely on your device. There is no Lantern backend, account or proxy.

What we collect

Nothing. Lantern does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Data that stays on your device

The server profiles you add, your settings and the traffic logs Lantern shows are kept on your device. Credentials such as tokens, API keys, passwords and private keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. Deleting the app removes this data.

Servers you connect to

When you connect to an MCP server, or sign in with an authorization server, Lantern talks to that server directly from your device. Whatever you send is governed by that server's own privacy policy. Lantern only connects to servers you add.

Local network

Lantern asks for local network access so it can reach MCP servers running on your network. It doesn't use that access for anything else.
